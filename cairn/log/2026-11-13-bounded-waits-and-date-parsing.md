# Bound the device wait, the redirect listener and the date parser

Three fixes from the family audit of 2026-11-13 (OAUTH-01, OAUTH-02, OAUTH-03), each reproduced by a test before it was touched.

**Device wait.** `Oauth20ClientStd::await_device_access_token` added the server's `expires_in` to `Instant::now()` and slept the server's `interval` before its first deadline check. `rfc8628::auth::Oauth20DeviceAuthSuccessParams` now has two clock-free accessors, `lifetime` (capped at one hour) and `poll_interval` (kept between 1 and 60 seconds), and the wait runs on them: it measures `start.elapsed()` against the lifetime instead of building a deadline, sleeps `interval.min(left)`, and raises the interval on `slow_down` with a saturating add. The raise itself is not capped, RFC 8628 section 3.5 asking for 5 more seconds each time; the time left bounds the sleep. The raw `expires_in` and `interval` fields are unchanged, so a caller that polls by itself has to move to the accessors to get the bounds. Moving the policy into an I/O-free coroutine that yields a wait was not done: it is a pending decision against "One poll per coroutine".

**Date parser.** `rfc6749::issue_access_token::parse_http_date` ran its day count in `u64`. It now runs in `i64` with `div_euclid` and `rem_euclid`, rejects a day zero and returns `None` for any instant before the epoch. Its signature is unchanged, and it stays in io-oauth (moving it to io-http is an open question).

**Redirect listener.** `client::await_redirect_on` loops on `accept` until a `GET` whose target is in origin form, whose path equals the path of the redirect URI and whose query carries `code` or `error`. Each connection has 5 seconds in total (the read timeout is re-armed with the time left before every read, so a peer sending a byte now and then gains nothing) and 8 KiB to deliver its request line; an interrupted read is retried. Anything else gets `HTTP/1.0 404 Not Found` and is dropped. The answer to the redirect is `200 OK` with "Authorization response received, you can close this page", written best effort: a browser that closed early no longer costs the caller a redirect it already holds. Requiring origin form closes a side hole: a target `@host/path` used to move the returned URL to another host. `Oauth20ClientStdError::InvalidRedirectRequest` had no producer left and was removed, the one break of this change; it also carried the raw request line, code and state included. Connections are served one at a time, so a silent one delays the redirect by up to 5 seconds. Not closed: the listener does not know the caller's state, so a request forged to the redirect path still ends the wait and fails the caller's state check.

Capabilities moved: `client` (Device polling loop and Redirect listener modified), `device-grant` (Bounded lifetime and interval added), `token-lifecycle` (Date parsing modified).

Verified: on the old code, `a_device_lifetime_beyond_the_clock_range_does_not_panic` panicked with "overflow when adding duration to instant", `a_device_interval_beyond_the_lifetime_does_not_outlast_it` timed out after 15 seconds, `http_date_before_the_epoch_returns_none`, `http_date_in_year_zero_returns_none` and `http_date_with_day_zero_returns_none` panicked with "attempt to subtract with overflow", `a_request_to_another_path_is_skipped`, `a_request_without_code_or_error_is_skipped`, `a_request_that_is_not_a_get_is_skipped` and `a_target_outside_origin_form_is_skipped` returned the stray request, `a_request_line_over_the_cap_is_skipped` and `a_silent_connection_is_dropped_after_the_read_timeout` timed out, and `a_redirect_carrying_an_error_is_not_answered_as_a_success` read "Authorization succeeded!". All pass now, with the clamp tests of the two accessors and two controls on the date parser (the RFC 9110 example and the epoch). The device tests reach a plain `http://` token endpoint on the loopback interface, the silent-connection test takes the 5 seconds of the timeout. `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy -D warnings` over `--all-features --all-targets`, `--no-default-features`, `--no-default-features --features client` and `--no-default-features --features rustls-ring,jwt-bearer` clean, `cargo build --no-default-features` (no_std) clean, `cargo test --all-features` passed (43 unit, 10 doc).
