# base64 runs on base64-codec

The base64 crate gave way to base64-codec 0.1 (`alloc`), as the family decided on 2026-11-03. src/rfc7636/pkce.rs and src/rfc7523/assertion.rs each encode through a private `base64url` helper (URL alphabet, unpadded), for the `S256` challenge, the three JWS parts and `x5t_from_der`; the output is unchanged. The codec reads the CPU token the application installed, the portable baseline otherwise; the library never detects. The jwt-bearer example decodes its PEM body through the codec too. pimalaya-stream is now required at 0.3.1 (rustls 0.23.46, RUSTSEC-2026-0346 to 0349), and the lock takes io-proxy 0.1.1, so no base64 crate is left in the tree.

Capabilities moved: none.

Verified: `cargo build --no-default-features` (no_std) and `cargo check --no-default-features --features jwt-bearer` clean, `cargo clippy --all-features --all-targets` clean, `cargo test --all-features` passed (22), among them the new `s256_challenge_matches_the_rfc_example` (RFC 7636 Appendix B, whose challenge holds a `-`) and the existing `x5t_from_der` known answer, `cargo deny check` ok.
